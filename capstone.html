GEOG 6161 / CAPSTONE

Summit County Fuels Treatment Dashboard

An applied GIS workflow for tracking and communicating fuels treatment activities in Summit County, Utah.

Overview

This capstone developed a workflow for collecting, processing, and displaying fuels treatment information for Summit County Lands & Natural Resources. The project connects field submissions with a map-based dashboard.

Workflow

  • Collect treatment polygons and associated shapefile attachments using Survey123.
  • Process submissions with a Python workflow for spatial data preparation.
  • Display treatment activity in an ArcGIS Dashboard covering 2020 to the present.
