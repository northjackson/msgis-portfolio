GEOG 6160 / SPATIAL DATA SCIENCE

Utah Streamflow Regime Classification

Using basin characteristics and flow metrics to distinguish streamflow regimes across Utah.

Research question

Can a spatial data science workflow classify Utah streamflow basins by regime and communicate prediction uncertainty?

Methods

The project used a Random Forest classifier with basin attributes and hydrologic metrics, including basin area, geographic location, mean annual flow, April–July flow fraction, and peak day of year.

Results

Model evaluation produced an approximate AUC of 0.885 with a random split and 0.744 with spatial validation, illustrating the importance of geographic generalization.
